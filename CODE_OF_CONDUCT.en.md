# VK Desktop Community Code of Conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

## Our commitment

VK Desktop participants, contributors, and maintainers are committed to a safe,
welcoming, and professional environment. Everyone participating in good faith
should be able to propose ideas, ask questions, report bugs, and discuss changes
without harassment or discrimination.

Proposals and contributions are evaluated according to their technical merit,
alignment with project goals, and quality of interaction—not a participant's
identity, background, experience, age, disability, appearance, nationality,
language, beliefs, gender, sexual orientation, or other personal status.

## Community standards

Encouraged behavior includes:

- respectful, focused discussion;
- constructive criticism that explains the reasoning and possible solutions;
- welcoming participants with different levels of experience;
- accepting feedback, acknowledging mistakes, and correcting them;
- protecting security, privacy, and intellectual property; and
- making decisions in the interests of the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- insulting, threatening, harassing, or discriminating against participants;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- personal attacks, deliberate provocation, or persistent disruption;
- publishing another person's private or confidential information without
  permission;
- spam, malicious code, phishing, or social engineering;
- impersonating the author, a maintainer, VK, or another organization;
- misappropriating another person's code, design, ideas, or authorship;
- pressuring maintainers to accept a contribution, grant permission, or
  transfer rights;
- retaliating against a good-faith report of misconduct; or
- submitting knowingly false or manipulative reports.

## Scope

This Code applies in the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and
other official VK Desktop spaces. It also applies in public spaces when someone
officially represents the project, uses an official project account, or speaks
on behalf of the community.

Personal disagreements outside this scope are not governed by this Code.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this Code consistently and
proportionately. Depending on the nature, severity, and recurrence of a
violation, maintainers may:

1. request correction or removal of inappropriate material;
2. issue a formal warning;
3. temporarily restrict participation in project spaces;
4. remove content, close a discussion, or reject a contribution; or
5. permanently ban a participant for a serious or repeated violation.

Decisions should be based on available evidence and conduct within the scope of
this Code. A maintainer directly involved in a conflict should, where practical,
not be the sole person reviewing the report.

## Reporting a violation

Reports are accepted confidentially through
[Telegram @ilushadevz](https://t.me/ilushadevz?direct). Whenever possible,
include:

- a link to the relevant discussion or material;
- a concise description of what happened;
- dates and the sequence of events; and
- available evidence and information about possible witnesses.

Do not publish tokens, passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive data
in public Issues. Report information will be disclosed only as necessary to
review the matter and take action, to the extent reasonably possible.
